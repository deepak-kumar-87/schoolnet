How to use this prototype

  1. Explore the dashboard, map and charts. All current records are labelled demonstration data.
  2. Use “Report an Issue” to create a report. You get a reference number such as RSA-2026-00001.
  3. Use “Track a Report” (or ask the chatbot) with your reference number to see its status history.
  4. Reports are saved only in this browser (localStorage). They are not sent to any government department or other residents.
  5. The chat button at the bottom-right opens the demo assistant. It uses simple local keyword rules, not a generative AI model.
